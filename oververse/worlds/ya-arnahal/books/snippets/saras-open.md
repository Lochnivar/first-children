"Papa Bear's Here!" little Ann squealed as she bolted through the sturdy wooden door into the kitchen of the Ratkowski homestead.  The door was thankfully sturdy enough to handle all the enthusiasm a QWST-enabled eight-year-old could generate.

Sarah winced as the door slammed against the wall behind.  With QWST-enhanced enthusiasm came the equally enhanced muscles and not even Sarah had learned to effectively modulate her new strenght, let alone an excited eight-year-old.  Luckily for the hinges and the wall, the System provided governor protocols in little Ann's child package which limited Ann's effective strength to something like one and a half times what a typical eight-year-old Earth human child could generate.  So even at full enthusiasm levels, Ann mrely left dents and scuffs in the wall rather than holes or damaged hinges.

"Ann Ratkowski!"  Sarah had to work at keeping the sterness in her voice.  "You are not a wild near-wolf.  And that's Marshall Pelask!  Bruin don't like to be called 'bears'!"

Sarah's gaze might have given even basilisks pause, but it bounced off Ann with little effect as the little girl ran off to let the rest of the family know.

Her mother shook her head and checked the heads up display (HUD) in the lower left corner of her vision.  A blue dot had just appeared at the extreme range of her detection. 

Just how far out had Ann been to have seen the marshall with her level 3 child senses?  Sarah sighed as she imagined the talk that she woudl have to have with Ann about how far she was allowed to roam from the homestead.  Maple Valley might not have ravaging hordes of monsters, but it did have it share of wild animals like near-wolves and ash bears that would find Ann a tasty snack.

But, that was a time for later.  Now, Sarah needed to prepare to receive visitors.  Ann would let Jake know, or Sarah could ping him directly if her daughter didn't.
