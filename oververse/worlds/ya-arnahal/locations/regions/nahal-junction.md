## Geography
**Corrected:** Nahal Junction does *not* sit at the twin-river confluence — that's [Kitzigen Falls](../../geography/climate-and-geology.md), a distinct location down at the escarpment. Nahal Junction instead sits at the **western** terminus of the main arterial road, in the fertile land *between* the twin rivers (not on either river directly). A second road forms a T-intersection at Nahal Junction, running **north-south** to settlements built directly on each of the two rivers (one north, one south). The main arterial road continues **east** from Nahal Junction, crossing the escarpment near Kitzigen Falls, into the trunk-river backcountry — consistent with the escarpment "facing east" already established in the geology material, a good fit rather than a coincidence.

Likely reading, not yet fully confirmed: new arrivals from the Cross land at Nahal Junction first — the safe, dense, administrative anchor — then travel east down the arterial road, through the escarpment threshold at Kitzigen Falls, into the harsher backcountry (Maple Valley, Green's Grant). See [world bible — Geography](../../../ya-arnahal.md) and [Geology & Climate](../../geography/climate-and-geology.md).

## Culture & Society
LGA administrative seat — claims office, homesteader package distribution point. Densest mesh on the planet; QWST here behaves close to the trope LitRPG default (near-continuous sync), in contrast to the backcountry.

## History
Named for the land-grant/allotment root of "Nahal," not a river confluence it doesn't actually sit on. The etymology still holds: it occupies the fertile land *between* the twin rivers — flanked by both rather than sitting where they merge — which is arguably an even stronger fit for "the best land is the water-adjacent land" than a single confluence point would be. See [world bible — Naming](../../../ya-arnahal.md).

## QWAN / QWST Integration
Densest node concentration on Ya'arnahal. Law enforcement and mesh density both radiate outward from here; backcountry claims can go months without contact with either.
