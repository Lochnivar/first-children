## Geography
**No maintained road reaches this region.** The main arterial road from Nahal Junction ends at Kitzigen Falls — not enough backcountry traffic to justify cutting and keeping one open beyond that point. From the Falls on, the single trunk river — too shallow for real ships, but capable of shallow-draft rafts and barges, real cargo capacity — is the real transport backbone; backcountry fans out along tributary creeks off the main channel, reached by river or by informal, unmaintained overland trail. Roughly half a dozen trading posts serve as informal social/information hubs for deep claimants — most plausibly sited directly on the river rather than any road. See [world bible — Geography](../../../ya-arnahal.md) and [Ecology](../../../ya-arnahal.md) for Neomaples and Yukon/mountain-register flora/fauna.

Reached from Nahal Junction via the main arterial road as far as **Kitzigen Falls** — the easiest of three passes (Schweddy Pass to the north, steep but manageable; Yanike Pass to the far south, nearly impassable) — then by river or trail beyond. See [Geology & Climate — The Passes](../../geography/climate-and-geology.md) for the full picture; Kitzigen Falls is a genuine mode-change point (road to river/trail), not just a terrain threshold, and worth remembering as the region's only reliable chokepoint if a scene ever needs it compromised or impassable.

## Culture & Society
Sparse, scattered claimants. Contact with Nahal Junction and with each other can go months between. Trading posts are the only reliable node-density spikes outside the capital.

## History
Later-settled than the twin-river strip north of the Junction — "latecomer's ground." The protagonist's claim is up a tributary here.

## QWAN / QWST Integration
The defining region for the world's core mechanic: QWST runs standalone here, caching state and reconciling opportunistically at the next node contact (trading post, neighboring claimant, trip to the Junction) rather than going dark. Claim registration is the network-dependent operation that makes this region's isolation load-bearing rather than just atmospheric — see [world bible — The System, Reframed](../../../ya-arnahal.md).

## Notable Locations
- **Trading posts (≈6, unnamed/undetailed):** informal hubs, the only regular node-density spikes
- **Maple Valley:** confirmed in prose (`books/snippets/saras-open.md`) — the specific tributary valley where the Ratkowski homestead sits; the place the working title refers to. Has near-wolves and ash bears (ordinary wild-animal danger) but not "ravaging hordes of monsters" — consistent with the claim-jumper-fear-over-hordes design principle
- **Green's Grant:** ~~a grassland pocket in the glacier-scoured lower valley~~ **corrected** — actually a grassland clearing in the *upper* reaches of Maple Valley, within the ancient unglaciated woodland, functioning as a rest stop on the main arterial road between Nahal Junction and Kitzigen Falls. See [Geology & Climate](../../geography/climate-and-geology.md). This means Maple Valley isn't uniformly harsh backcountry — its upper reaches touch the old-growth, road-adjacent country, while the Ratkowski homestead sits further down in the younger, glacier-scoured ground this file otherwise describes

## Regional Hazard
A katabatic-style cold-snap — air forced over the bounding Himalayan-scale peaks, chilled past what a normal descent would rewarm — produces sudden, severe cold distinct from ordinary winter hardship. Foreshadowable and recognizable to old-timers, not to newcomers; see [Geology & Climate](../../geography/climate-and-geology.md) for the full mechanism. Good candidate for a scene demonstrating earned competence.
