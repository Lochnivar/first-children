## Background
Refugee-settler track, not economic-settler track: displaced during Earth's Summer of Chaos — the chaotic, wall-less period of cascading disaster predating the Commission (see [Earth AI Refresher](../../../earth/AI-REFRESHER.md)) — possibly a Blitz-style evacuation/fostering displacement. Family separation is the formative trauma. Off-world placement to Ya'arnahal came later, once Commission-era Amoskeag terminus infrastructure existed to route people through the Cross — displacement first, relocation second, two separate events, not one.

**Three separated life-stages, not two:** (1) childhood/young-adult displacement and family separation on Earth during the Summer of Chaos, (2) decades lived as a baseline, unenhanced Earther — 20–30 years — presumably somewhere in the Commission-era system before emigrating, (3) taking the LGA land grant as an established adult, receiving QWST for the first time as part of the homesteader package. The enhancement is recent relative to her whole life; the trauma that set her on this path is not. This gives her purist-leaning distrust of totalizing systems real weight — it's an adult's considered wariness, not reflexive unfamiliarity, and she has a genuine baseline to compare against when she resents leaning on the System.

Arrived solo per the original premise, but the story's present finds her married/settled with a family at the Ratkowski homestead in **Maple Valley** — a specific place within the trunk-river backcountry below Nahal Junction, up a tributary (geographically and narratively the latecomer's ground; see [world bible — Geography](../../../ya-arnahal.md)). Arrival and narrative present are not the same moment — treat the original "solo emigrant claims land" beat as backstory, not the opening scene, unless flashback is the intent.
