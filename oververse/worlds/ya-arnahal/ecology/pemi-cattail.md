**Category:** Flora — Ya'arnahal

## Naming

"Pemi" follows the same settler-shorthand convention established for the North Fork ("the Pemigewasset") — a familiar Earth-sourced fragment applied to something the narrator either can't or won't render by whatever its original/official name is (see [AI Refresher — Tone](../meta/AI_REFRESHER.md)). Not necessarily the same plant the North Fork nickname was coined for, just the same naming instinct applied a second time.

## Role in Pollination

A flowering native plant hummingbees are drawn to — part of the real ecological pollination relationship documented under [Hummingbee — Ecological Role](hummingbee.md).

## Extract and Its Use

Cattail extract is enzymatically active — applied by hummingbees to their own winter-lantern casings, it preferentially breaks down structural seams rather than dissolving material uniformly (see [Hummingbee — Emergence and Harvestable Seams](hummingbee.md)). Settlers use the same extract in processing harvested casings into [Ya'arnahal Glass](yaarnahal-glass.md).

## Secondary Uses

- **Surface disinfectant** — mildly acidic; too harsh for open wounds, but useful on tools, slate molds, and stone counters.
- **Scrubbing/polishing** — the plant's fibrous outer husk works as a scrubbing sponge or coarse polishing pad.
