# Ecology Index

Flora and fauna of Ya'arnahal, detailed enough to warrant their own file.

## Megaflora
- [The Cottage Oak](cottage-oak.md) (*Quercus domesticus*) — corky-heartwood tree settlers excavate for shelter; northern/upland latitudes

## Fauna
- [Lumberjacks](lumberjacks.md) — gopher–jackrabbit cross that dens in Cottage Oak heartwood, wasting cork settlers want; low-key recurring nuisance, not a threat
- [Hummingbee](hummingbee.md) — avian/reptilian hummingbird-analogue filling the pollinator niche insects can't sustain here; builds a communal winter-lantern casing whose discarded shells become the raw material for [Ya'arnahal Glass](yaarnahal-glass.md)
- *To be developed* — near-wolves, ash bears named in prose (`../books/snippets/saras-open.md`) but not yet detailed here; a vole/lemming-style small mammal filling the keystone-prey-base niche (see [Planetary Geography — Ectotherm Exclusion](../geography/planetary-geography.md)); one apex predator deliberately held in reserve as myth, not encounter (see [world bible](../../ya-arnahal.md))

## Other Flora
- Neomaples — tappable, cross-species emergency food source, quiet irony against the LGA's "nothing worth exploiting here" framing (see [world bible — Ecology](../../ya-arnahal.md))
- [Wicker Willow](wicker-willow.md) — hierarchical fiber-bundle tree, not actually related to Earth willow; structural lumber, wicker-craft, and linen-like textile fiber from one species. One of the lower valley's most economically load-bearing plants
- [Milknut](milknut.md) — cold-hardy nut/fruit tree; antifreeze fluid doubling as anti-scurvy nutrition, seed roasts into a coffee-substitute stimulant. Fluid drink and coffee-substitute product names still open
- [Pemi Cattail](pemi-cattail.md) — riverbank flowering plant; enzymatic extract central to Hummingbee biology and Ya'arnahal Glass processing, plus disinfectant/scrubbing secondary uses
- [Rainbow Heather](rainbow-heather.md) — common groundcover, mundane except for soil-chemistry-linked bloom color; doubles as an informal nitrogen soil test once understood. First on-page domestic-mystery scene (Sarah and Nick's flower bed)

## Craft & Economy
- [Ya'arnahal Glass](yaarnahal-glass.md) — settler glazing process built entirely from discarded Hummingbee casings; first real characterization of the Moreau family (a courtyard/atrium homestead design)
