# Ya'arnahal Glass (Hummingbee-Pane Glazing)

**Category:** Craft/economy — settler processing chain, downstream of [Hummingbee](hummingbee.md) and [Pemi Cattail](pemi-cattail.md)
**Related:** [Hummingbee](hummingbee.md), [Pemi Cattail](pemi-cattail.md), [Wicker Willow](wicker-willow.md), [The Cottage Oak](cottage-oak.md), [The Moreaus](../characters/README.md), [The White Fangs](climate-and-geology.md)

---

## Core Concept

Translucent, durable glazing for the Moreau family's central courtyard — an enclosed atrium serving as both greenhouse and shared living space. The courtyard design is a deliberate departure from the standard log-cabin form, chosen because a sealed central buffer zone is a better structural response to Ya'arnahal's winter severity (White Fangs, hard winters generally) than a single-envelope cabin footprint. Whether the courtyard/atrium approach is a Moreau-specific design or a broader regional adaptation remains open.

## Source Material

Harvested from discarded Hummingbee winter-lantern casings — see [Hummingbee](hummingbee.md) for the full biological mechanism. The casing is naturally pre-structured into seam-bounded plates by the colony's own segmented construction process, and those seams are what the harvesting and processing methods exploit throughout.

## Settler Processing

1. **Gather** — discarded, abandoned winter casings scavenged from known hummingbee shelter sites (settlers removing old shells incidentally does the colony a favor, clearing the site for its next cycle — a mutualism neither species intended); [Pemi Cattail](pemi-cattail.md) stalks harvested along the riverbank.
2. **Break** — casings pried/fractured along their natural construction seams into rough, workable plate-sized fragments — closer to quarrying slate than to melting raw material into slurry.
3. **Soften & Cast** — cattail extract applied to loosen and finish plate edges as needed; material strained or fitted into flat slate molds, or set directly into small [Wicker Willow](wicker-willow.md) lattice sashes.
4. **Heat-Cure** — gentle hearth heat drives off the extract's remaining moisture. As it dries, the same hyper-crosslinking reaction that permanently hardens the hummingbee's abandoned shell finishes the settler-cast piece, curing it into a solid, waterproof, amber-tinted pane.

## Refinement

The mold-side face of a cured pane bakes into a cloudy, mineralized release skin, scraped away with river silt/sand and buffed clear with wet leather and cork dust (a small tie-in to [Cottage Oak's](cottage-oak.md) own byproduct economy). Over-processing — too much soaking, reprocessing, or excess heat — scorches or over-vitrifies a piece into brittle, unusable stone; good glazing takes real, learnable skill in timing and heat control, consistent with every other material in Ya'arnahal's ecology rewarding competence and punishing carelessness.

## Architectural Integration

The courtyard canopy uses a Wicker Willow lattice grid (kumiko-style) — small, individually-framed panes rather than large sheets. This modularity means heavy snow load or wind flex cracks at most a single pane rather than the whole roof; a broken pane is popped out, a replacement cast and cured, and slid back in. Frames are sealed with a cork-dust-and-plant-wax paste. Early homesteads likely run a hybrid roof — tightly woven, sealed fiber panels alongside whatever glass panes exist yet — with glass gradually replacing fiber as harvest yield and casting skill improve over successive seasons.

## Narrative Function

Glazing quality ties directly to an off-page ecological variable nobody controls — a strong hummingbee season yields abundant, easily-sourced casings and a bright, sunny courtyard; a poor one leaves fewer shells to gather and a dimmer atrium. This gives the household a quiet, ambient barometer for how the broader season is going, legible without any character needing to remark on it. Gathering casings, harvesting cattail, breaking seams, and polishing panes are ordinary, grounded chores — ideal for the story's competence-earned, low-drama domestic register (see [Emotional Core](../README.md)), and a good source of small, character-building scenes (a parent teaching a child to read seam quality on an intact shell, the whole family working a good haul of casings together after a strong season).
