# Milknut

**Species type:** A cold-hardy nut/fruit-bearing tree, unrelated to true coconut palms in form — likely a non-palm growth habit suited to Ya'arnahal's climate — but functionally convergent in what its fruit does and how it's used.
**Category:** Flora — Ya'arnahal (region not yet specified)
**Related:** [Neomaples](../../ya-arnahal.md), [The Winter Scale / no-axial-tilt climate](climate-and-geology.md), [Ecology index](README.md)

## Structure

A large, hard-shelled nut/fruit with two functionally and chemically distinct internal components:

- **Outer fluid layer** — a sugar- and solute-dense liquid, functioning as both a freeze-protection mechanism for the plant and a nutritionally serious food/drink source for settlers. High solute concentration lowers the fluid's freezing point (the same real-world mechanism as antifreeze), and the tree likely also produces genuine antifreeze proteins that bind to forming ice crystals and halt their growth — a documented real strategy in cold-hardy plants (winter rye, some conifers). Also carries elevated vitamin C and calcium, both plausible extensions of the same cold-adaptation biochemistry (ascorbic acid is independently linked to freeze-tolerance in real plants, not just antioxidant/nutritional function) — giving the fluid genuine value against Ya'arnahal's real, historically-grounded scurvy risk for a homesteading population with limited fresh produce access over a hard winter.
- **Inner seed(s)** — bitter, inert in raw form, chemically distinct from the fluid layer. Contains a native precursor compound whose actual biological function is germination-acceleration: a "starter charge" that lets the seedling establish as fast as possible once conditions allow. This is a direct, earned consequence of Ya'arnahal's non-seasonal, tipping-point climate (see [The Winter Scale](climate-and-geology.md)) — no reliable calendar-based growing window to wait for, so seeds are under real selection pressure to move fast the instant conditions are viable. This precursor is not itself a stimulant.

## Processing

Roasting (or similar heat-based processing) breaks down the seed's native precursor compound; one of the resulting breakdown products is caffeine or a functionally equivalent stimulant. This mirrors real coffee processing closely — raw seed unpalatable and functionally inert to humans, only becoming useful/stimulating through a specific, learned preparation technique. The human use is incidental/opportunistic — discovered through trial and error, not something the plant "evolved for."

## Use

- **Fluid** — consumed directly, or reduced (boiled down toward a concentrated, semi-caramelized form) and reconstituted with water into its own comfort drink. **Explicitly a distinct product from Neomaple's** — occupies a similar comfort-drink niche but isn't the same thing under a different name; still needs its own identity/name.
- **Seed** — roasted and prepared as a coffee substitute, genuine stimulant effect via the processing-derived caffeine analogue.

## Naming

**Milknut** — the tree and its raw nut. Plain, functional, settler-coined, same register as Cottage Oak and the White Fangs: names what the thing gives you (a nut that yields a nutritious fluid), not a scientific description. The reconstituted fluid drink and the roasted seed/coffee-substitute are still open — could inherit the root ("milknut cream," "milknut coffee") or earn their own separate names later, on demand, not preemptively (see [AI Refresher — Tone](../meta/AI_REFRESHER.md)).
