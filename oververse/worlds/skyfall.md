# Skyfall

**Status:** Framework-Level Summary - Proposed World (Concept Development)
**Category:** Solo Prime-Author Creation, FC-Infiltrated
**Related:** [First Children](../cosmology/first-children.md), [Genus Draconis](../races/draconis/genus.md), [../FRAMEWORK.md](../../FRAMEWORK.md)

## Overview

Skyfall is a Greater-Dragon-built world later crashed by **the Four Pantheons** — 54 First Children organized like a deck of cards (four suits of thirteen, plus two wild cards) who seeded their repository species there as open, legitimate worshippers. Competitive magic-school rivalry among those mortal factions, inherited from the Pantheons' own suit rivalries, built dungeons that spawned monsters past any ability to contain them and overran the ground. Survivors live in floating magi-tech cities, raiding the surface by airship. Underneath the mortal survival story is a dragon-tier tragedy: the Greater Dragons went silent rather than fight a war that would destroy the world regardless of outcome, striking an unexplained bargain with the Pantheons to stay out entirely — leaving their Lesser Dragon children to split into a faction that still has faith and a faction that calls the silence betrayal.

**Core Theme:** What's owed to the abandoned, and whether restraint from the powerful reads as wisdom or complicity from below.

## Framework Elements Demonstrated

### FC Infiltration, Not Creation
- **Not the world's creator:** The Four Pantheons did not make Skyfall — the Greater Dragons did, as Prime Author creations (see [Genus Draconis](../races/draconis/genus.md)). The Pantheons are trespassers who seeded a repository species and an open religion into someone else's world.
- **Open Cosmology, FC-Adjacent:** Mortals know the Four Pantheons as gods and worship them outright — closer to Hegemon/Stratara's "mortals aware of the players" than Iolanu/Brenneth's hidden-or-unknown-creator models, but the worship is sincere religion, not acknowledgment of a game.
- **Sibling Tension at the Edge:** The Pantheons' open claim over the Lesser Dragons pushes against genus.md's default FC/Greater-Dragon protocol ("tolerate, don't confront... respect mandatory or face eternal war"). Skyfall is a near-breaking outlier in that relationship, not routine sibling friction.

### The Four Pantheons (54 First Children)
- Structured like a card deck: four suits of thirteen plus two wild cards. Not an exhaustive census of Hypatia's First Children — just the cohort that organized into this particular game. Suits encode an FC-level genre-of-fun (Spades/Hard Mode, Clubs/Sandbox Mode, Diamonds/Systems Mode, Hearts/Social Sim Mode), never mortal-legible — worshippers only ever see the fractured, individuated output, never the organizing logic. **Domains are a shared grid, not per-suit lists:** 13 comparative-mythology-grounded domains (Seasons, War/Glory/Honor/Freedom, the classical elements, etc.), each suit taking its own genre's angle on the same 13 rows — see [four-pantheons-roster.md](skyfall/four-pantheons-roster.md) for the full grid.
- Motive is recreational, not strategic: worlds are playgrounds to bored First Children generally. Contrast Hegemon/Stratara, where personality-heavy FC run a self-aware contested game; the Four Pantheons are personality-*light* by design — arrived naive, ancient in knowledge but nearly blank in selfhood — which is precisely why this particular game (becoming someone through worship, rather than competing on an agenda you already have) appealed to them and not to Hegemon/Stratara's Strategists.
- **Boredom explains the arrival, not the 500-year residency.** Per the general law that First Children need an ongoing stake or attention drifts (see [First Children](../cosmology/first-children.md#investment-and-attention-general-law) — Malachor's autopilot Church is the demonstrating case), boredom alone predicts the Pantheons should have wandered off by now. They haven't, because the hobby turned up something none of them expected — see The Free Anchor, below. Came for the hobby, stayed for the experiment.

### Worship as Waveform Interference ("We create our gods," literalized)
- FC communicate through **waveform interference** (see [First Children](../cosmology/first-children.md)). Skyfall takes that mechanic literally: sustained, focused mortal worship is interference at the layer FC operate on, and a naive First Child acquires an actual personality from it, not just a reputation. The gods are fully real and powerful — what mortals authored was their selves, not their existence.
- **Crystallization, decoupled from rank.** A god's selfhood accumulates from worship and can rise or slip as worship shifts — a live variable, not static lore, which opens the possibility of a deliberate belief campaign reshaping a god on purpose. This no longer tracks rank position (rank is now a shared-domain axis, not a popularity ladder — see The Grid in [skyfall/README.md](skyfall/README.md)), so every one of the 52 gets full worship-sketch treatment regardless of where it sits. **The mechanism made visible:** Tohuel (the Weaver) and Bohuel (the Unweaver), the two Wild Cards, are crystallization's rise and slip personified — her mending is a god's rank strengthening, his tearing is a god's rank slipping, and storms/the Rainbow are the same process at physical scale.
- **Mortals as unwitting co-authors:** the Pantheons' possessive claim over the Lesser Dragons is plausibly downstream of what Skyfall's own earliest, frightened worshippers projected onto blank gods — not innate divine cruelty. A distinct framework demonstration: mortals as accidental co-creators of the very gods who wronged them, rather than pure victims of an external will.

### The Free Anchor (Why They Stayed)
- Quantumancy's core limitation is the Persistence Problem: holding a collapsed state against drift requires an **Anchor** — ordinarily an artifact, or the First Child's own sustained attention (see [Quantum Foundation](../metaphysics/quantum-foundation.md), [QSON](../metaphysics/qson.md)). What the Pantheons stumbled onto mid-hobby is that sustained, mass, sincere worship *functions as* an Anchor — a distributed, self-sustaining one that costs its own First Child nothing. None of the 54 arrived understanding this; they found it the way you find something by leaving a hobby running long enough.
- This is the actual engine of long-term investment, distinct from the original recreational motive. A naive, personality-light First Child who's just discovered a way to hold reality in place *for free, forever, without spending personal attention* has found something no amount of boredom will make them walk away from — not because the discovery is understood, but because it's the first thing any of them has ever had that doesn't cost.
- **Playing god as variable-tuning:** any *direct* Quantumancy the Pantheons spent on Skyfall would cost them the very attention the free Anchor lets them keep — so the one lever they can still pull without undoing the discovery is which prayers get answered, which miracles land, which cults get encouraged. Performing devotion isn't customer service; it's the only knob left. Keep this fiddling, not methodology — the 54 are prodding something they don't understand, not running controlled experiments with hypotheses. That distinction is what keeps them naive rather than sliding into Hegemon/Stratara-style calculating Strategists.

### The Skyfall Bargain
- Greater Dragons concluded direct war with the Pantheons would destroy the world regardless of victor (consistent with Greater Dragons being functionally undefeatable sibling-equals to FC, not subordinates).
- Rather than fight or evacuate, they struck an unspecified trade with the Pantheons in exchange for non-interference — content deliberately reserved, unexplained even to their own Lesser Dragon children. Demonstrates the "creator/protector goes offstage for unexplained reasons" pattern already present elsewhere in the framework, applied to a Second Children lineage rather than a First Child.

### Lesser Dragon Civil War
- Eastern (faith the Greater Dragons' restraint is wisdom, grounded in fear of provoking the Pantheons without backup) vs. Western (disillusioned, blames Eastern's restraint for ongoing deaths, wants war on the monsters and/or the Pantheons' mortal cult).
- A live, present-tense accusation of responsibility for ongoing deaths — not retrospective blame — which is why shared extinction doesn't suppress the infighting.

## Development Status

**Resolved:**
- **The Catastrophe was cascade, not sabotage** — driven by inherited genre one-upmanship. The Four Pantheons' own suit rivalries are unglamorous gamer-culture pettiness (Hard Mode players sneering at Social Sim players, Sandbox purists butting heads with Systems clockwork-minded rivals) — genre-snobbery doesn't require developed selfhood, so even personality-light FC have it for free. Rival mage-schools inherited that contempt from their divine patrons and tried to out-do each other's dungeons to win the argument, not to end the world. Nobody sabotaged anything; everybody was showing off. See [The Free Anchor](#the-free-anchor-why-they-stayed), above, for why the Pantheons had a lever (which cults they encouraged) but no reason to want this outcome.
- **The domain grid** — replaces the original per-suit domain lists; see [skyfall/README.md](skyfall/README.md) and [comparative-mythology-worksheet.md](skyfall/comparative-mythology-worksheet.md).
- **The Sun, exempted** — Aurelion the Firstborn (Genus Draconis) is believed to have withdrawn into the sun after the Bargain; started as Eastern Lesser Dragon doctrine, now universal, deliberately not narrator-confirmed. See [skyfall/README.md — The Sun-King Myth](skyfall/README.md#the-sun-king-myth).
- **How crystallization actually surfaces in-story** — Tohuel/Bohuel, see above.

**Questions for Further Development:**
- Float-tech and float-mechanism origin (inherited dragon infrastructure vs. survivor-engineered)
- Raid-mechanism specifics and how they map to city wealth/capability
- The Skyfall Bargain's actual terms (deliberately reserved as a future hook) — open question whether Tohuel/Bohuel, as Skyfall's instigators, were also the ones who brokered it

**Distinct from Delverium:** Delverium is a self-aware LitRPG dungeon experiment (Zorathar's meritocracy test). Skyfall's dungeons are a catastrophic, accidental, industrial-scale disaster with no game framing — keep the contrast sharp so the two "dungeon worlds" don't blur.
