# Comparative Mythology Worksheet — Remapping the Four Pantheons

**Status:** Working document — diagnostic tool, not yet canon
**Related:** [Skyfall README](README.md), [Four Pantheons Roster](four-pantheons-roster.md), [Pantheon Sketches](pantheon-sketches.md)

## Method

The original 52-card portfolio was generated top-down from "genre of fun" (Hard/Sandbox/Systems/Social Sim), then individuated into mortal-facing domains. That produced a suit like Spades that reads as a war-and-death monoculture even where the underlying mechanic is sound, and produced several domains (Guilds, Measurement, Hoarding, Executioners) that feel like RPG-splatbook subcategories rather than something a scared or hopeful mortal would actually build a shrine to.

This worksheet inverts the method: start from the universal domains real-world comparative mythology organizes around — the things virtually every historical pantheon has a god for — and ask what each of the four houses' genre-lens does with the same domain. Where a domain already splits cleanly across multiple houses in the existing roster, that's confirmation the design is sound. Where a domain is missing, monopolized by one house, or thinly represented, that's an actionable gap.

## Confirmed Working (already split, or splitting in progress)

| Domain | Real-world grounding | Current Skyfall split |
|---|---|---|
| **Death & the Underworld** | Hades/Persephone (Greek), Hel (Norse), Osiris/Anubis (Egyptian), Ereshkigal (Mesopotamian), Yama (Vedic) | Spades = Motiel, battlefield finality · Diamonds = Koseb, the indifferent ledger · Hearts = Kinreth, competitive mourning · Clubs = Kemahar/Atzelon, pending reframe to necromancy-as-overgrowth |
| **Seasons & the Calendar** | Demeter/Persephone (Greek), Freyr (Norse harvest), Osiris as dying-and-rising grain god | Hearts = Ragash → Spring · Clubs = Gorenath → Summer · Diamonds = Pikudan → Autumn · Spades = Winter, proposed replacement for Qadriel |
| **Fate & Destiny** | Moirai (Greek Fates), Norns (Norse), Ananke (Greek necessity) | Diamonds = Britael, a contract that "executes automatically via reality-law, indifferent to whether signatories are alive" — fate as enforced system · Hearts = Ragash, literally "Fate-Pairing" already |
| **Trickery & Chance** | Loki, Hermes, Anansi, Coyote | Answered by the **Wild Cards** (Tohuel/Bohuel) rather than a suit-card — arguably the correct call, since tricksters are traditionally boundary-crossers who refuse categories anyway |

## Partially Working (present, but monopolized by one house)

| Domain | Real-world grounding | Current coverage | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| **War & Conflict** | Ares, Tyr, Sekhmet, Huitzilopochtli | Spades owns almost all of it: Harban (War), Gaduel (Dueling), Tebhel (Siege), Reshaq (Espionage) | No Diamonds (war-as-economics: embargo, supply lines — this is exactly what the proposed **Attrition** replacement for Sha'alon would be), no Hearts (war-as-court-politics, partially present in Bashanor's "subtle poison"), no Clubs (war-as-conquest-of-territory, partially present in Sharaq's "vector wind... without asking permission") |
| **Wealth & Fortune** | Plutus, Fortuna, Lakshmi, Ebisu | Diamonds owns nearly all of it: Ashiron, Heteron, Sekharon, Yasharon | No Spades angle (plunder/spoils-of-war — pairs naturally with the Attrition/war-economics thread above) |
| **Justice & Law** | Themis/Dike, Ma'at, Forseti, Yama-as-judge | Split two ways already: Spades = Dinath (martial/tribunal law), Diamonds = Takanor (civil/bureaucratic law) | No Hearts (court-of-public-opinion, partially present in Bashanor), no Clubs (the wilderness's amoral fairness, partially present in Sadehol's "rewards humility, not skill") |
| **Love & Marriage** | Aphrodite, Freya, Hathor, Hymen | Mostly well-distributed: Hearts = Ahavel (romantic love) + Dodiel (marriage), Clubs = Perath (procreative fertility), Diamonds = Britael (marriage-as-binding-contract) | No Spades angle (love tested/scarred by hardship — the closest existing material is Hearts' own Re'utan, "friendship forged in shared trauma," which arguably belongs to Spades' side of this domain instead) |

## Resolved

| Domain | Real-world grounding | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| **Sky & Storm** | Zeus, Thor, Set, Indra | Split, not merged: **Sun exempted from the pantheon entirely** — believed home of Aurelion the Firstborn (Greater Dragon, see [Skyfall README — The Sun-King Myth](README.md#the-sun-king-myth)), started as Eastern Lesser Dragon doctrine, now universal mortal/dragon folklore, deliberately not narrator-confirmed. **Storm** still needs a home — proposed for Diamonds as "the one variable no system can account for" (raid-window/trade-route disruption), not yet drafted into the roster. Yabesh (Clubs, existing) keeps the drought/patience angle.

## Genuine Gaps

| Domain | Real-world grounding | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|
| **Wisdom & Knowledge** | Athena, Thoth, Odin, Saraswati | Not represented anywhere. Notably relevant to this setting specifically: the scavenger-engineers maintaining inherited dragon-grade infrastructure they don't fully understand is already a load-bearing premise (see Ma'ashel's worshippers) — a god of lost/inherited/half-understood knowledge would land hard here. |
| **Health, Sickness & Healing** | Asclepius, Sekhmet, Eir | Currently only exists via Qadriel (Plague/Famine) — **which the Winter swap is about to delete.** Once that happens this domain has zero coverage. Needs a new home: candidates are Hearts (healing as an act of care, the sickbed vigil) or Diamonds (healing as triage math — scarce medicine rationed by the same cold arithmetic as everything else in that suit, which also ties nicely to the raid-for-resources premise). |
| **Hearth & Home** | Hestia/Vesta, Roman Lares/Penates, ancestor-shrine traditions worldwide | Not represented — closest adjacent is Hearts' Dodiel (favoritism within a family) and Diamonds' Chevelon (boundary-survey), neither of which is really "the home as protected/sacred space." Lower priority than the two above; no strong tie yet to Skyfall's specific premise. |

*(Minor/optional, not fully explored: Boundaries & Thresholds — Janus, Hermes, Ganesha — loosely present via Chevelon; Sleep/Dreams/Madness — Hypnos, Nyx — currently absent and not clearly load-bearing for this setting.)*

---

## Open Decisions

1. Where does **Health/Healing** land once Qadriel is replaced by Winter — Hearts (care/vigil) or Diamonds (triage math)?
2. Is **Wisdom & Knowledge** worth adding as a new domain, given how well it fits the "inherited tech nobody fully understands" premise already established for Ma'ashel's cult?
3. Should **War, Wealth, and Justice** be deliberately redistributed across the other three houses (Attrition/plunder into Diamonds and Spades, court-politics-as-war into Hearts, territorial-conquest into Clubs) — this would do double duty, decluttering Spades' war-monoculture *and* giving the other three houses sharper, more mythologically legible identities?
4. Is **Hearth & Home** worth pursuing, or is it a gap without enough narrative payoff to earn a slot?
5. **Storm** still needs to actually be drafted as a card (proposed: Diamonds, "the one variable no system can account for") — resolved in direction, not yet in text.
