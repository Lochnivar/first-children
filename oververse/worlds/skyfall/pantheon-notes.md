### Rank 10 principle (user — locked lean)

**Biomes are containers, not substrates.** Rank 10 is not "about the sand / the trees / the rocks / the mud" as stuff. Each suit reads **what that biome holds or does as mode weather**:

| Biome | Suit | Not the stuff | What it **contains / does** (mode) |
|-------|------|---------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Forests** | Spades | Not timber taxonomy | **Predatory cover** — formation erased; ground that hunts the disciplined line (Hard Mode restriction) |
| **Deserts** | Hearts | Not sand grains | **Mystique / sparse address** — stories on the wind, unanswered word, night-fire *we* (Social) |
| **Mountains** | Diamonds | **Not stacks of rock / load-pile architecture** | **Depths & resources** — caves, mines, seams, assay, what the mass holds (Systems entry of the deep) |
| **Swamps** | Clubs | Not mud chemistry | **Unemptiable return** — basin that comes back, growth that won't stay drained (Sandbox) |

So the Mountains reframe is not a special plea — it's **applying the same rule the row already uses**. Old Tzuron (span/gravity/foundation-stone) was treating the mountain as **building material / structural pile**, which is substrate-logic — and that's why she slid into Aretzon (Earth as foundation/base). Correct Diamonds read: mountain as **vault of the deep**, entry for contents.

**Aretzon fence under principle:** Earth Jack is element-as-foundation (base that doesn't move). Mountains 10 is biome-as-contents (what the high mass holds underneath). Different rank axes; no need to share "immovable rock woman" identity.
